## Publication rule
No artist is approved for the live website merely because a Spotify search result showed fewer than ten monthly listeners. Spotify monthly listeners change over a rolling 28-day window. Before publishing, recheck the exact artist ID and current count (0–9), document a dated screenshot or independently reproducible source, check recent musical activity, and seek credible evidence of human performance/production without generative AI. Do not infer “AI-free” merely from named band members.
