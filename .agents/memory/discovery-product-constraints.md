Less Than 10 Listeners is for active human bands that started with fewer than 10 Spotify monthly listeners. The user says “Definitely exclude ai”, “Active only”, and “every band gets its turn before anyone repeats”.

**Why:** These are explicit product requirements, not incidental demo data.

**How to apply:** Real eligibility requires a manual review. Fairness must apply across visitors, not just a local carousel. Do not describe browsing a demo reel as the persistent fair-selection system.

The user says “Mobile first designs the carousel needs to be the hero with the winner graphic dominating the screen” and “Remove all the small texts must be super clean classify minimalism”. The approved direction is premium black and muted gold with original sculptural artwork, not Spotify art or artist photos.

**Why:** The user explicitly prioritizes the dominant coverflow and a minimal interface.

**How to apply:** Preserve the art-led mobile composition when adding features; put necessary detailed explanations in unobtrusive accessible surfaces.

The user's iPhone feedback rejects “blurry simplistic colour blobs”. They want richly detailed sculptural artwork, dramatic dark depth, a gold-framed winner, and bold compact white “LESS THAN 10” with metallic gold “LISTENERS”.

**Why:** Direct iPhone feedback says the previous visuals fell far short of the approved cinematic reference.

**How to apply:** Preserve sharp sculptural detail rather than diffuse blob gradients. Quick flicks should carry through several panels and coast for several seconds, while slow drags stay controllable. Mechanical ticks should follow panel crossings with a distinct final stop; audio needs a user gesture and an accessible mute control.

The user selected “STYLE 8 NEON CYBER MODERN ELECTRIC” as the primary carousel artwork: enormous legible three-dimensional numerals in transparent dark glass/acrylic, glowing electric-blue outer edges and orange-red inner tubes, black futuristic architecture, subtle vertical light strips and wet reflective floor. Cover all digits 0–9, using the band's starting listener count so its discovery number stays unchanged after later growth.

**Why:** The user explicitly selected this number-led aesthetic instead of the previous flowing abstract artwork. The numeral represents the discovery snapshot, not current listeners.

**How to apply:** Keep a coherent luminous neon treatment, real sculptural depth and rich reflections, not flat text or generic gradients. If captioning inside the artwork, use “MONTHLY LISTENERS WHEN DISCOVERED”; do not mislabel the starting number as current.

The user approves the spinning physics, momentum and mechanical clicks and asks to preserve them while changing the artwork. The approved lower layout is position dots, elegant serif band name, starting-monthly-listener and outbound-click lines, one wide stationary Spotify button, then a thin divider and only Love, Share and QR controls. No genre, track, dates or instructions in the primary lower section.

**Why:** The user explicitly approved this final vertical-phone arrangement and says the existing artwork and reel behavior are loved. The mockup's “8” is illustrative only, never permission to seed clicks.

**How to apply:** Preserve the approved composition unless the user explicitly requests a visual change. Keep demo scope discreetly visible; real band starting counts require verification, and outbound actions must never be represented as actual listeners or listens.

Keep fictional demonstration figures distinct from real measurements. Spotify redirects are click-throughs, not evidence of listens. Monthly listener snapshots must be manually verified; do not claim the standard Spotify API provides them.

**Why:** The user explicitly prohibits misleading real-world claims.

**How to apply:** Label demo records, dates, links, and sample figures. Never seed fabricated engagement and display it as actual activity.

Do not publish publicly without the user's permission.

**Why:** The user asked to keep this project unpublished.

**How to apply:** Preview and verify locally; do not publish or suggest publishing during recovery work.
